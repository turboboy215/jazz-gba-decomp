# Music: songs, internal names and where they are used

All music is GAX 3.01 data by **Martin Schioeler**. Every song plays at 21,025 Hz with 7 channels
(the credits song has 6). There are 9 songs and one shared sound-effect bank. The raw table is regenerated by
`tools/gax_songs.py` → `docs/songs_generated.md`.

Each song's title string (`"name" © Martin Schioeler`) is part of the GAX sequence data,
just before the song's settings struct. These are **not** game text, which is why they're excluded from
`docs/strings.tsv`.

## Internal title vs actual use

The internal titles don't describe where the songs play. "Observed" comes from listening to GSF rips of
the game (user's notes, Oct 2026). "Code" comes from references in the ROM.

## Level table (`0x083D39D8`, 25 records × 0x4C bytes)

Each record starts with its song pointer. Other fields seen so far: `+0x14` tileset, `+0x18`/`+0x1C`
more graphics pointers, `+0x40` level data, `+0x48` 256-colour BG palette (512 bytes). Field names are
still provisional.

Level names come from the level-select menu strings (`083DB59C…`, 24 names in table order). Record #19
has no menu entry.

**"castle" and "snow" look swapped**: "castle" plays on the snow planet (Stage 3, ICEBOX3, matching the
listening notes), and "snow" plays on Stage 5, whose story text mentions "way through the castle" and which
ends with Dark Shell. "spacelevel" covers the title and first level, and "mainmusic" covers the space
station / character levels (Stage 2, Zoe, Prof. Lapen, Spaz). Confirm Stage 5's theme in-game.

Patterns:

* The single "mainmusic" levels between stages (#9 Prof. Lapen, #14 Spaz, #19 unnamed) use the space
  station tileset but the **next** stage's palette.
* All four `boss1` fights share one level-data block (`08060B20`) and the palette `083D4D44`.

## Tileset format (found so far)

`u16 tile_count; u16 bpp (= 8); u8 tiles[tile_count][64]`: raw, uncompressed 8bpp (256-colour)
8×8 tiles. Example: world A at `0822B100` has 951 tiles. Palettes are 256 × BGR555 at `+0x48` in the
level record. Map/metatile format: TODO.

## Ripping notes

The GAX copy in the multiboot client image (see `rom_map.md`) makes gaxtapper pick the wrong library. The workaround is to patch
ROM offset `0xF2CC` to 0 ([gaxtapper#17](https://github.com/loveemu/gaxtapper/issues/17)).
